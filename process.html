OUR 8-STEP RECYCLING PROCESS

Discover the complete circular scrap metal workflow. From customer enquiry and certified on-site alloy assessment to dispatch scheduling, calibrated weighbridge verification, high-capacity processing, and responsible secondary smelter recovery.

End-to-End Circular Lifecycle

CIRCULAR METALLURGY WORKFLOW AT A GLANCE

Our streamlined 8-step framework guarantees total alloy traceability, transparent weighbridge settlements, safety compliance, and maximum economic return for industrial scrap producers.

1. Scrap Enquiry 2. Material Assessment 3. Rate Estimation 4. Pickup Scheduling 5. Weighing & Verification 6. Sorting & Segregation 7. Processing & Recycling 8. Responsible Recovery
01
Step 1: Scrap Enquiry Initiation & Logistics Intake

The process begins when an industrial client, manufacturing facility, demolition contractor, or fabrication shop submits their scrap collection requirement. Clients can submit inquiries through our online portal, call our 24/7 industrial dispatch desk, or schedule a free on-site facility scrap audit. During this intake stage, we capture critical parameters including scrap origin, material category (ferrous, non-ferrous, superalloys, or cables), estimated tonnage, frequency (one-off warehouse clearance or continuous recurring generation), and container placement access.

Online Booking Portal 24/7 Dispatch Hotline Industrial Facility Audit Roll-off Bin Drop Planning
02
Step 2: Material Assessment Metallurgical & Lot Evaluation

Our certified metallurgists and commercial estimators evaluate the material category, alloy condition, contamination levels, and lot size. For high-value non-ferrous metals and specialty aerospace superalloys, we dispatch mobile technicians armed with handheld Thermo Scientific Niton XL5 Plus XRF spectrometers and optical emission spectroscopy (OES) devices. This on-site, non-destructive assay identifies elemental alloy composition within seconds (e.g., verifying 8% Nickel in 304 stainless steel or confirming 60% Nickel in Inconel 625), ensuring sellers receive the true intrinsic value of their material.

Handheld XRF Elemental Assay Physical Lot & Thickness Inspection Moisture & Oil Deduction Grading Hazardous & Sealed Cylinder Screening
03
Step 3: Rate Estimation Benchmark Pricing & Formal Quotation

Following material grading, we generate a transparent indicative quotation based on exact material specifications, physical assay purity, and prevailing London Metal Exchange (LME) and COMEX benchmark commodity indices. Our commercial pricing formulas clearly state base market rates, applicable high-tonnage volume bonuses (+3% to +8%), container logistics allowances, and any necessary processing deductions for un-sheared heavy structures. Clients receive a written quotation with a price-lock guarantee for up to 5 business days.

LME & COMEX Benchmark Linking Bulk Volume Tier Premiums Written Transparent Quote 5-Day Price Lock Guarantee
04
Step 4: Pickup Scheduling Logistics Coordination & Fleet Dispatch

Our logistics dispatch team coordinates timely scrap collection and equipment deployment tailored to your facility's operational schedule. Depending on scrap volume and density, we position 15-yard to 50-yard heavy-duty roll-off containers, leak-proof CNC swarf bins, flatbed semi-trailers, or deploy mobile knuckle-boom crane trucks with orange-peel grapples for immediate live-loading. For heavy structural demolitions, our OSHA-trained mobile torch-cutting crews perform on-site dismantling before transport.

15-50 Yd Roll-Off Containers Watertight CNC Swarf Bins GPS Live-Tracked Fleet Dispatch On-Site Mobile Cutting Crews
05
Step 5: Weighing and Verification Certified Scales & Radiation Safety

Upon arrival at our recycling yard, every transport passes through automated Ludlum 375P portal radiation detection gates that screen 100% of incoming payloads for gamma and radioactive anomalies before gate entry. The vehicle proceeds onto our dual 80-foot computerized pitless truck weighbridges, calibrated by state weights and measures authorities. Automated license plate recognition (ALPR) and multi-angle high-resolution cameras record the load. An instant Gross Weight ticket is generated, followed by Tare Weight recording immediately after unloading.

Ludlum 375P Radiation Screeners Dual 80-Ft Computerized Scales High-Res Inbound Photo Records Time-Stamped Gross/Tare Tickets
06
Step 6: Sorting and Segregation Classification by Metal Family & Grade

Once tipped onto our reinforced 45-acre concrete receiving pads, heavy materials are segregated by category and metallurgical specification. Liebherr LH 60 material handlers equipped with electro-magnets, orange-peel grapples, and hydraulic shears separate heavy ferrous plate from lighter sheet stampings. Non-ferrous lots are directed to clean covered vaults, where trained sorters segregate red brass from yellow brass, clean copper wire from insulated cables, and aerospace titanium from chrome-moly alloys.

Electro-Magnetic Separation Liebherr Material Handlers Dedicated Covered Vault Storage ISRI Specification Classification
07
Step 7: Processing and Recycling Industrial Sizing, Shredding & Densification

Materials undergo specialized mechanical processing to convert raw scrap into furnace-ready charging feedstock:

  • 1,200-Ton Guillotine Shear: Cold-cuts oversized structural steel columns, ship girders, and pipeline segments to under 5 feet (ISRI 200/201 Heavy Melting Steel).
  • 4,000 HP Mega-Shredder: Crushes light mixed scrap and auto bodies at 600 RPM, passing through air cyclones to eliminate upholstery and foam fluff.
  • 3,000 RPM Eddy-Current Separator: Deflects aluminum and copper fragments away from inert plastics and residue.
  • Triple-Action Hydraulic Balers: Compact clean sheet stampings (busheling) into ultra-dense 1,200 kg/m³ charge cubes for electric arc furnaces.
  • Cable Granulation Line: Chops insulated wire into millimeter-sized granules, using air-density tables to yield 99.9% bare copper chops.
1,200-Ton Guillotine Shear 4,000 HP Shredder Line 3,000 RPM Eddy Current Triple-Action Balers Granulation & Air Tables
08
Step 8: Responsible Material Recovery Secondary Smelting & Sustainable Off-take

The final stage completes the circular economy cycle. Processed, high-density, lab-verified secondary metals are loaded for delivery to domestic electric arc furnace (EAF) steel mills, secondary aluminum smelters, and copper foundries. Using our deepwater Panamax vessel berths and direct rail spurs, consignments are shipped with full Certificates of Recycling, ESG Scope-3 emissions reduction calculations, and zero-waste-to-landfill manifests. By replacing virgin mined iron ore, bauxite, and copper concentrates, our recycling process conserves up to 95% of energy and prevents thousands of metric tons of greenhouse gas emissions.
