Business Introduction

SUSTAINABLE INDUSTRIAL SCRAP RECOVERY & RESPONSIBLE RECYCLING

Ferromax is an established market leader in industrial scrap metal collection, mechanical processing, and secondary commodity trading. For over three decades, we have partnered with manufacturing plants, automotive stamping facilities, fabrication yards, and civil demolition contractors to turn surplus metal streams into high-purity furnace-ready raw materials.

Our 45-acre deepwater harbor terminal features heavy hydraulic shearing, high-speed mega-shredding, automated eddy-current non-ferrous separation, and mobile XRF alloy spectroscopy. We are committed to zero-landfill industrial waste diversion, transparent digital weighbridge settlements, and strict environmental compliance across every consignment.
