SCRAP MATERIAL RATE CARD

Official industrial benchmark rate sheet. Our pricing models reflect global commodity exchange movements (LME & COMEX), regional electric arc furnace demand, verified alloy purity, and bulk contract volume incentives.

Market Fluctuation & Physical Quality Disclaimer

All rates displayed below represent indicative benchmark ranges for industrial receiving and commercial contracts. Metal scrap prices fluctuate continuously based on the London Metal Exchange (LME), COMEX, regional mill purchasing indices, and foreign exchange rates. Final settlement payouts are determined at the time of physical weighbridge scale recording, following spectroscopic elemental assay (XRF), and adjustment for allowable moisture, dross, or non-metallic attachments. Rates do not constitute a live streaming brokerage offer.
