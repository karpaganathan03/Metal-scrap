The Circular Methodology

OUR RECYCLING APPROACH

A disciplined four-pillar secondary metallurgy framework that guarantees maximum recovery purity and environmental sustainability.

01

Audit & Container Placement

On-site material audits identify all scrap generation points. We position custom-sized roll-off bins and swarf hoppers to streamline factory segregation at the source.

02

Screening & Weighbridge

Arriving loads pass continuous portal radiation detection. Certified 80-foot weighbridges capture encrypted weights and photo logs before physical triage begins.

03

Mechanical Sizing & Sorting

1,200-ton hydraulic shearing, 4,000 HP shredding, high-intensity magnetic drums, and 3,000 RPM eddy-current rotors separate ferrous from non-ferrous with 99.2% accuracy.

04

Secondary Smelter Off-Take

Finished charging bundles, busheling cubes, and copper chops are dispatched directly to secondary electric arc mills and smelters via vessel berth, rail car, or container.
