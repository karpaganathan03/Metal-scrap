Heavy Melting Steel (HMS 1 & 2)

Cut wrought iron and heavy steel scrap. HMS 1 has a minimum wall thickness of 1/4 inch (6.35 mm); HMS 2 comprises 1/8" to 1/4" thickness including vehicle frames, piping, and cut fabrications.

Scrap Classification: ISRI 200, 201, 202, 204
Typical Applications: Rebar, beams, structural steel
Collection Method: 30 to 40 Cu. Yd. Roll-Off Bins
Material Evaluation: Scale tare, thickness gauge, ≤0.5% dirt

Scrap Classification: ISRI 231 (5ft) & ISRI 232 (3ft)
Typical Applications: High-specification foundry casting
Collection Method: Flatbed crane trailers, 40-yd luggers
