Regulatory & Environmental Policy

SCRAP ACCEPTANCE & COMPLIANCE POLICY

Strict environmental governance, OSHA guidelines, and EPA regulations dictate material ingestion across our 45-acre deepwater marine facility.

Materials Approved for Scale Ingestion

The following items are purchased directly upon digital weighbridge certification:

  • Heavy Structural Steel & Plate: I-beams, girders, cut demolition frames, structural channels, and heavy steel plate exceeding 6mm thickness.
  • Bare Bright Copper Wire & Busbars: High-voltage copper transmission conductors, substation busbars, clean copper pipe, and brass plumbing scrap.
  • Clean Architectural & Cast Aluminium: 6063 window extrusions, de-rubbered alloy wheels, manufacturing sheet offcuts, and aluminium transmission casings.
  • Austenitic Stainless & Superalloys: 304 and 316 stainless solids, chemical pipe, flanges, Monel 400, and Inconel 625/718 aerospace alloys.
  • Insulated Power Cables & Electric Motors: Armored transmission cable, machine tool harness, copper building wire, and intact motor stators.
  • Drained & Certified Mechanical Scrap: Heavy industrial engine blocks, gearboxes, and hydraulic rams with verified zero oil residue.
  • De-Valved & Opened Commercial Tanks: Steel drums and tanks with inspection cutouts confirming zero chemical residue or pressure.

Strictly Prohibited & Restricted Items

The following hazardous, explosive, or radioactive items are rejected at our facility gates:

  • Sealed or Pressurized Containers: Un-valved propane cylinders, fire extinguishers, aerosol cans, and closed pressure vessels without a certified inspection hole.
  • Radioactive or Orphan Sources: Any scrap triggering our gate-mounted Ludlum 375P dual-pillar gamma radiation portal monitors will be immediately isolated.
  • Asbestos-Coated Materials: Pipe lagging, structural brake bands, acoustic panels, and transite boards contaminated with asbestos fibers.
  • PCB-Contaminated Equipment: Oil-filled high-voltage electrical transformers, capacitors, fluorescent lighting ballasts, and hydraulic units with PCB fluids.
  • Un-Drained Fuel Tanks & Explosives: Automotive gasoline tanks, un-punctured fuel cells, munitions, military ordnance, and commercial fireworks.
  • Medical & Biological Waste: Hypodermic sharps, biological laboratory scrap, pathological containers, and untreated hospital waste.
  • Municipal Garbage & Dirt: Non-metallic refuse, timber, concrete chunks, soil, household plastics, and tires mixed into scrap loads.
Yard Ingestion Workflow

HOW TO DELIVER SCRAP TO FERROMAX TERMINAL

Follow our streamlined 4-step B2B delivery workflow for immediate scale turnaround, zero-delay unloading, and same-day electronic payments.

01

Grade Segregation

Keep high-value non-ferrous metals (copper, brass, aluminium) segregated from general ferrous steel to maximize commercial payout without mixed deductions.

02

Drain Oils & Fluids

Drain all engine oils, coolants, and fuels from vehicle assemblies, hydraulic cylinders, and gearboxes. Cut open and vent any commercial vessels.

03

Dual Scale Ingestion

Enter Gate 4 for automated optical license-plate capture, radiation portal scan, and certified gross weighing on our dual 80-ft digital weighbridges.

04

Immediate Settlement

Tare out after grapple/crane unloading to receive automated electronic weight tickets. Payouts are transferred same-day via wire, RTGS, or ACH.
